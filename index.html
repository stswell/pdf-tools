PDF Tools

Merge, split, compress, convert, edit and sign PDFs. Everything runs in your browser, so documents never leave this computer.

How your files stay private

Your files are never uploaded

Many online PDF tools send your document to their company's servers to do the work. This one doesn't. When you choose a PDF, your web browser opens it from your computer and does all the work itself: merging, compressing, signing, OCR. Your document is never sent to STS's servers or to any other company.

Results go straight to your Downloads folder

Nothing is stored online, and there's no account to sign in to. Closing the tab clears your files from the browser's memory. The only thing kept is a signature you choose to "Remember on this computer", and that stays in this browser only.

What the page does download

When the page opens, it downloads the tools themselves (program code and fonts) from public download sites (cdnjs, unpkg, jsDelivr and Google Fonts). OCR also downloads its text-recognition model the first time you use it. These are downloads to your computer. Your documents are never sent to those sites.

Check it yourself

Press F12, open the Network tab, then use any tool. You'll see the tools and fonts coming in, and none of your documents going out.
